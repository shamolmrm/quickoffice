# QuickOffice — App Guide (shohoj bhashay)

> **Ek kothay:** QuickOffice holo choto byabsha, dokan, freelancer, office ar NGO-r jonno "pocket office".
> Phone diye **invoice, quotation, money receipt, chithi, seal ar CV** banano, ar **ke koto taka dilo / baki ache** tar hishab rakha jay.

---

## 1. App ta kader jonno?

| Ke | Ki kaje lagbe |
|---|---|
| Dokan / wholesale byabsha | Customer-ke invoice, money receipt, delivery challan |
| Freelancer / service | Client-ke quotation, tarpor invoice, payment track |
| Office / HR | Appointment, experience, salary certificate, NOC letter |
| Choto company | Purchase order, seal, letterhead, report |
| Chakri-prarthi | CV / Resume banano |

---

## 2. Prothombar app khulle

1. **"Set up your business"** page ashbe.
2. Byabsha-r **naam** din (eta lagbei). Baki gula chaile pore-o deya jay:
   - **Logo** (gallery theke chhobi)
   - **Signature** (angul diye shoi kore)
   - **Seal** (app-er bhitore seal baniye)
   - Thikana, phone, email, website
   - Tax / BIN / VAT number
   - **Currency** (৳, $, ₹, €, £, SAR, AED …)
   - **Payment details** (bKash, bank) — invoice-e QR code shoho ashbe
3. **Start** chapun → Dashboard khulbe.

👉 Ekbar dile shob invoice, receipt, chithi-te nije theke boshe jay.

## 4. Document gula (mul kaj)

### 4.1 Invoice (bill)
**Kibhabe banaben:**
1. ➕ chapun → **Invoice**
2. **Date** ar **Due date** din (7 / 15 / 30 din button ache)
3. **Bill to** → client bachhai korun ba **New** chepe notun client
4. **Add item** → jinish-er naam, poriman (qty), unit (bag, kg, pcs…), dam (rate), tax %
5. Dorkar hole **Discount** (taka ba %) ar **Shipping** charge
6. **Save & preview** → sundor PDF ready

**Shortcut — ✨ AI fill:** upore "AI fill" chepe likhun:
> "Rahim Traders, 10 bosta cement 550 taka, 2 ton rod 85000, 5% discount"

AI nije client ar item boshiye dibe.

### 4.2 Quotation (dam-er prostab)
Invoice-er motoi. Client raji hole page-e **"Make invoice"** chaplei invoice hoye jay — abar likhte hoy na.

### 4.3 Money Receipt (taka pawar rashid)
- Shudhu **taka-r poriman**, **kar kach theke**, **kiser jonno** ar **payment method** (Cash, bKash, Nagad, Bank…) din.
- PDF-e "Received with thanks from…" ar **taka kothay lekha** (jemon "Taka Five Thousand Only") ashe.

### 4.4 Purchase Order (PO)
Supplier-ke maal order dewar kagoj.

### 4.5 Delivery Challan
Maal pathanor kagoj — dam chhara, shudhu jinish ar poriman. Invoice ba PO theke ek chap-e banano jay.

## 5. Invoice banano-r por ki kora jay

Invoice khulle ei button gula paben:

| Button | Kaj |
|---|---|
| 👁 **Preview** | PDF dekha, print, phone-e save |
| 📤 **Share PDF** | WhatsApp, Email, Messenger-e pathano |
| 💵 **Record payment** | Client taka dile likhe rakha (puro ba ashik). Por-e money receipt-o banano jay |
| 🔔 **Remind** | Client-ke WhatsApp-e baki taka-r message (normal ba AI diye, Bangla/English) |
| 🚚 **Challan** | Ei invoice theke delivery challan |
| ⋮ menu | Duplicate (copy), Cancel, Delete |

**Status nije bodlay:**
- 🟠 **Unpaid** — taka ashe ni
- 🔵 **Partly paid** — kichu ashe-che
- 🟢 **Paid** — puro taka paisi (PDF-e "PAID" stamp)
- 🔴 **Overdue** — due date par hoye geche

## 6. PDF design

- **3 ta design:** Modern, Classic, Minimal (More → Business → Invoice design)
- **Brand colour:** nijer pochhonder rong
- PDF-e thake: logo, byabsha-r info, item table, total, **taka kothay**, terms, payment QR, **shoi + seal**, page number

## 7. AI Letter Maker (More → AI Letters)

**12 dhoroner chithi:**
Appointment · Offer · Experience Certificate · Salary Certificate · NOC · Leave Application · Resignation · Warning · Termination · Recommendation · Business Proposal · Custom

**Kibhabe:**
1. Chithi-r dhoron bachhai korun
2. **Details**-e choto kore likhun (naam, pod, tarikh, beton…)
3. **English** ba **বাংলা** bachhai → **Write**
4. AI subject ar puro chithi likhe dibe, chaile edit korun
5. **Save & preview PDF** → letterhead, tarikh, reference no., shoi ar seal shoho

---

## 8. Seal Maker (More → Seal Maker)

- **Gol, Oval ba Chokona** seal
- Upore company-r naam, niche shohor, majhe ★ / APPROVED / PAID / RECEIVED ityadi
- Kali-r rong: nil, beguni, lal, sobuj, kalo
- **"Use on documents"** → shob PDF-e boshbe
- **"Share PNG"** → transparent chhobi hishebe onno jaygay use

---

## 9. CV Maker (More → CV Maker)

- Chhobi, naam, pod, phone, email, thikana
- Profile, experience, education, skills, languages, training, reference
- **✨ AI** button: profile summary sundor kore likhe dey
- Rong bachhai → **Create CV PDF**
- Lekha save thake, porer bar ese edit kora jay

---

## 10. Clients ar Products

**Clients:**
- Invoice banale client nije save hoy
- Client-er page-e: mot bill, **koto baki**, shob document, **Call** ar **WhatsApp** button

**Products (More → Products):**
- Invoice-e lekha jinish nije save hoy, dam-o mone rakhe
- Porer bar naam-er prothom akkhor likhlei suggestion ashe

---

## 11. Reports (More → Reports)

- Bochhor onujayi: **mot invoice**, **mot taka elo**, **ekhon koto baki**
- 12 mash-er chart
- **Top 5 client**
- **Ke ke taka dey ni** list
- **Excel (CSV) export** — accountant-ke pathano jay

## 12. Reminder / Notification

Invoice-er due date thakle app nije mone koriye dey:
- **Due date-er 1 din age** ar **due date-er din** (sokal 10 tay)
- Prothombar notification permission chaile **Allow** din

## 13. Free vs Premium

| | Free | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Shob document ar tool | ✅ | ✅ |
| PDF-e "Made with QuickOffice" lekha | Thake | ❌ Nei |
| Ad | Ache (share/save-er age choto ad) | ❌ Nei |
| AI | Ekta ad dekhle 15 minute | Shob shomoy |

Dui ta alada app hishebe Play Store-e deya jabe: **QuickOffice** (free) ar **QuickOffice Premium** (paid).
Free app-er bhitore-o Premium kena jabe (lifetime ba monthly).

## 16. Developer-er jonno (apnar jonno note)

| Ki | Kothay |
|---|---|
| Project folder | `D:\devloper\app\QuickOffice` |
| Test APK | `release\QuickOffice-FREE.apk`, `release\QuickOffice-PREMIUM.apk` |
| Icon ar banner | `store\` folder |
| AI key ar ad ID | `keys.json` (Play Store-e dewar age asol AdMob ID boshate hobe) |
| Upload key (Play Store) | `android\upload-keystore.jks` + `android\key.properties` → **Drive-e backup rakhun** |
| Play Store build | `build_release.ps1` chalale AAB toiri hobe |

**Play Store-e dewar age korte hobe:**
1. AdMob-e asol ad ID baniye `keys.json`-e boshano
2. Play Console-e `premium_lifetime` ar `premium_monthly` product banano
3. Privacy policy link
4. Screenshot ar listing lekha (bollei baniye dibo)
